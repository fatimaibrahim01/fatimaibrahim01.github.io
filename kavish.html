Kavish by SCINOSA

Social Media Marketing & Product Promotion

ScopeSocial Media Strategy & Product Marketing
PlatformsInstagram • Facebook
TimelineApproximately 4 months

1. The Project

Kavish by SCINOSA was a social media and product-marketing project created around handmade wooden products produced through SCINOSA’s vocational work. The goal was to build an online presence for the products, introduce them to a wider audience, and use social media as a channel for organic promotion and sales. I worked on the project for around four months, managing the social media presence from setting up the platforms and planning the content to publishing and promoting the products.

2. My role

I handled the social media side of the project independently, including setting up the Instagram and Facebook presence, developing the content direction, planning content, creating posts, publishing content, and promoting the products organically. My focus was not only on making the pages look active, but on using social media to present the products clearly, reach potential customers, and support actual sales.

3. What I did

Social Media Setup

I created and set up the Instagram and Facebook presence for Kavish, giving the products a dedicated space to be presented and promoted online.

Strategy & Content Planning

I developed the social media direction and content plan around the products, focusing on how to present the handmade pieces and communicate their value to potential customers.

Content Creation

I created social media content for the products, including visuals and promotional posts designed to showcase the wooden frames and encourage people to enquire about them.

Organic Marketing

I promoted the products organically through social media, focusing on reaching relevant audiences and generating interest without relying on paid advertising.

Product Promotion & Sales

The social media activity was connected directly to the goal of generating product interest and sales. The project resulted in individual product sales as well as a larger order enquiry.

4. Outcome

The project generated direct sales through the social media presence. During the project, 4 pieces were sold, and an order for 100 pieces was received. This experience showed me how social media can be used not only to build a product presence, but also to create a path from content and promotion to real customer interest and sales.
